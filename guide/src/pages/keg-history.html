The Keg History page lists every keg that has been kicked, the most recently kicked first. Use it to look back at what you have poured and when.

Read the history

Each row shows:

  • Beer: the beer that was in the keg.
  • Tap: the last tap the keg was on, or a dash if it never went on a tap.
  • Type and Size: the kind of keg and how much it held.
  • Tapped: the day the keg went on a tap, or a dash if it never did.
  • Kicked: the day the keg was kicked.

Delete a keg from the history

Deleting a keg from the history is permanent and cannot be undone.

  1. In the keg's row, select Delete.
  2. In the Delete keg dialog, select Delete.

The Display Settings page changes how the TV display looks: its title, colors, fonts, layout and which beer details it shows. It also sets the volume units the admin app uses. A preview beside the form shows the TV with your changes before you save them.

What each setting does

  • Title: the heading at the top of the TV, up to 60 characters. Leave it empty for no title.
  • Theme: Dark or Light colors on the TV. This does not change the admin app's own look, which you choose at the bottom of its side panel.
  • Units: US (gallons, with pours in fluid ounces) or Metric (litres, with pours in millilitres). This changes the admin app only; the TV shows no volumes.
  • Page seconds: when the TV has more than one page, how long each page shows before the next, from 5 to 300 seconds.
  • Layout: Grid (cards, up to 8 a page), Vertical list, or Side by side (taps and on deck next to each other).
  • Title alignment, Title font and Title color: where the title sits (Left, Center or Right) and how it looks.
  • Text font and Text color: how the beer names and details look.
  • Tap details: tick the details each tap card shows: Style, ABV, IBU, SRM and Description.

Change the display

  1. Change any of the settings. The TV preview shows the result straight away, but nothing changes on the TV yet.
  2. If the preview has more than one page, use Previous and Next to look at each one.
  3. Select Save. The TV picks up the change within about 10 seconds, with no need to touch it.

Go back to the theme's color

While a color setting uses the theme's color, it says "Theme color" under it.

  1. Next to Title color or Text color, select Reset.
  2. Select Save.
